Ship Supply Services in Namibia

Ship Chandler Namibia supplies a complete range of marine stores for merchant vessels, including:

Provisions & Bonded Stores

Fresh, chilled, frozen and dry provisions for vessel crews and galley operations.

  • Fresh fruits & vegetables
  • Prime halal beef, poultry & lamb
  • Fresh & frozen fish and seafood
  • Dairy products, cheeses & eggs
  • International tobacco brands & cigars
  • Soft drinks & energy beverages

Deck & Engine Stores

Comprehensive deck consumables, technical materials, and engine department supplies.

  • Mooring ropes & synthetic hawsers
  • Wire ropes & heavy towing lines
  • Shackles, turnbuckles & lifting accessories
  • Filters, gaskets & bearings
  • Hand and power tools
  • Engine-room chemicals & absorbents

Cabin, Safety & Technical

Everyday consumables, safety gear, and technical spare parts for smooth operations.

  • Bed linens, blankets & pillows
  • Commercial kitchen cookware & utensils
  • SOLAS lifejackets & lifebuoy rings
  • Safety helmets, boots & protective coveralls
  • Navigational incandescent & LED lamps
  • Pump replacement spares & compressor parts
